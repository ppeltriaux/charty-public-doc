# Charty

**Visual routine charts for kids — calm, predictable, ad-free.**

Charty helps children (primarily ages 4–10, including kids with ASD and other developmental needs) follow daily routines with visual cards, earn ⭐ for completed tasks, and unlock rewards. Built by a parent of a child with ASD, designed around the things that matter most for that family: predictability, sensory safety, no surprises.

---

## What it does

- 📋 **Routines** — Build your own charts, or start from six built-in templates (Morning, Bedtime, After School, Homework, Chores, Bath time).
- ⭐ **Points & rewards** — Earn ⭐ for finished tasks. Trade ⭐ for the rewards you define (screen time, treats, an outing, anything).
- 🧩 **Picture-puzzle reward** — Spend ⭐ to reveal pieces of a real wooden-style jigsaw of a hand-illustrated scene or a family photo. Drag them onto the board.
- 🎉 **11 celebration styles** — Confetti, Rainbow Burst, Stars Shower, Balloons Up, Fireworks, Calm Wave, Dinosaur Stomp, Toy Cars Zoom, Robot Bounce, Big Trucks Roll, Sirens & Lights, plus a "calm celebrations only" global override for sensory-overwhelm days.
- ⏲️ **Routine timer** — Standalone 1–60 min countdown with parent-set end behaviour (gentle pulse + chime, full celebration, alarm).
- 🖨️ **Paper print** — Multi-page A4 PDF with tickable boxes; AirPrint to your printer or save to Files.
- 🦖 **190+ illustrated icons** across 10 categories (Hygiene, Meals, Dressing, School, Bedtime, Play, Chores, Movement, Emotions, Travel) — Google's Noto Emoji set plus original Charty illustrations (toothbrush, table, toast, tablet, phone, and the puzzle / celebration art packs).

## Designed for special-needs families

- 🧘 **Calm celebrations only** override for sensory-sensitive days
- 🔊 **Sound + haptics + animations** all individually toggleable
- ♿ **Reduce Motion** respected on every animation
- 🗣️ **VoiceOver** support and Dynamic Type up to AX5
- 👆 **88 pt minimum hit targets** on every child-facing element
- 🔒 **Parent gate** (PIN or Face ID) protects Settings + chart edits from accidental kid taps
- 🌐 **Universal** — runs on iPhone and iPad, with a 2-column grid layout on iPad
- 🌍 **7 languages built-in** — English, French, Spanish, Italian, German, Brazilian and European Portuguese
- ☁️ **Optional iCloud sync** — same charts, rewards, points, and puzzle progress on every device in the family

## Privacy

**Charty collects nothing.** No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third-party SDKs that phone home. Your data stays on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, your data syncs through your own iCloud private database — Charty's developer cannot see it.
