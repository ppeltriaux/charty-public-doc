## Frequently asked

### How do I unlock parent settings?

The gear icon (top right of the Rewards tab) opens the parent gate. Unlock with your PIN or Face ID. If you haven't set a PIN yet, the gate is unlocked by default — set one in **Settings → Security**.

### My child can't see the Rewards button I added.

Rewards are kid-visible on the Rewards tab. If yours isn't showing, check that you saved it in **Settings → Rewards admin → Manage rewards**. The points cost might also be higher than the current balance — try lowering it temporarily to confirm.

### How do I reset the points balance?

**Settings → Data → Reset points balance.** Confirms in a centred alert. Earned-points history isn't affected.

### Can I share a chart between two iPads / iPhones?

Yes. Turn on **Settings → Data → iCloud sync** on each device (signed into the same iCloud account). Your charts, rewards, points, and puzzle progress sync through your own private iCloud database — Charty's developer can't see it.

### What's free, and what's Charty Plus?

The free version includes **2 charts, up to 5 tasks per chart, and 1 child** — a complete routine. **Charty Plus** (a one-time purchase, no subscription) unlocks unlimited charts and tasks, multiple children, recorded parent voice, parent insights, per-task timers, the Apple Watch app and interactive widget, and every theme.

### How do I unlock Charty Plus?

**Settings → Charty Plus → Unlock** (behind the parent gate). You can also reach it by tapping the upgrade prompt that appears when you hit a free-tier limit.

### I already paid for Charty — do I have to pay again?

**No.** If you owned Charty when it was a paid app, Charty Plus unlocks automatically and for free — you're never asked to pay again.

### I bought Charty Plus on another device / reinstalled — how do I get it back?

**Settings → Charty Plus → Restore Purchases.** It's a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID and is covered by Family Sharing.

### The celebration is too much for my child today.

Turn on **Settings → Sensory → Calm celebrations only**. Every chart's celebration is clamped to **Calm Wave** at **Gentle** intensity until you turn it back off.

### How do I print a chart?

Open the chart in the Build tab → scroll down → **Print or share as PDF** → iOS share sheet → AirPrint (or Save to Files / Mail / Messages). Long charts paginate at 8 tasks per page.

### Can I export my data?

**Settings → Data → Export sessions as CSV.** This emits a CSV of completed sessions + redemptions you can share with a therapist or OT.

### My child finished a puzzle — can they do it again?

Yes. **Settings → Picture puzzle → Picture source** → tap any bundled scene or pick a photo to start a fresh puzzle. The previous finished puzzle is archived in **Past completed puzzles** on the play page.

## Bug reports

When you email, please include:

- iPhone / iPad model (e.g., iPhone 15)
- iOS version (Settings → General → About → Software Version)
- Charty version (Settings → About → Version)
- What you tapped that triggered the bug
- Screenshot if possible
